# Fictional proposal: assisted drafts for an internal help desk

This is an original, synthetic learning fixture written for this repository. The organization, measurements, incidents, and costs are invented; they are not a real pilot or product result. The fixture and its three source notes may be used and modified within this project. No third-party documents are included.

## Decision requested

Run a six-week pilot in which an AI tool drafts answers for routine internal help-desk requests. Five reviewers may use the drafts, but a person must inspect and send every response. Limit the pilot to password-reset instructions and equipment-request status; exclude account-specific decisions and sensitive employee data. Approve a maximum pilot spend of 500 fictional currency units.

## Proposed reasons

1. A small synthetic exercise showed median handling time falling from 12 to 9 minutes for eligible requests, suggesting a possible 25% time reduction.
2. Human approval before sending should reduce the chance that an incorrect draft reaches an employee.
3. A capped pilot could reveal whether drafting is useful before broader rollout.

## Assumptions and limits

- The 40-request exercise represents future demand; this has not been established.
- Reviewers will catch account-specific errors consistently; the exercise does not prove that.
- The 500-unit cap covers provider, implementation, and review time; the cost note does not yet establish this.
