# PILOT-01 — synthetic exercise observations

Original fixture authored for this repository; all observations are invented. Passage IDs are stable within this example.

## PILOT-01:P1 — sample and eligibility

Forty fictional internal help-desk requests were sampled. Twenty-four fit the two proposed routine categories. Sixteen required account-specific judgment or contained information outside the proposed scope. No draft was sent automatically.

## PILOT-01:P2 — time measurement

For the 24 eligible requests, the synthetic median handling time was 12 minutes without drafts and 9 minutes with drafts and human edits. The note does not include confidence intervals, reviewer learning effects, or a future workload estimate.

## PILOT-01:P3 — draft usefulness

Eighteen of the 24 eligible drafts were usable after human edits; six were discarded. This is an exercise result, not evidence that the same rate will hold in a six-week pilot.
