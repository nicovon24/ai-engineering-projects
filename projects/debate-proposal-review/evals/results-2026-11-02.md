## Independent qualitative sample

The independent review inspected one completed synthetic review per locale. In both, opposing arguments covered costs, error/privacy controls, and uncertain efficiency. Each synthesis named five questions with specific needed evidence, including a cost/volume model, sensitive-data validation, approval logs, and learning effects.

The Spanish supporting prose claimed that human approval guarantees accuracy although the fixture did not measure missed reviewer errors. That assertion was absent from its linked structured claims, so the ledger could not flag it. Other prose described an incompletely costed budget as feasible or unvalidated controls as robust. The interface must present the analysis as fallible, and reviewers must inspect argument prose as well as the claim ledger.

This is a two-review qualitative sample, not measured counterargument recall. Human inter-reviewer agreement was not measured; the independent reviewer is an AI agent requested as Sol high, with effective runtime identity unconfirmed. No human agreement or production reliability result is claimed. Broader bilingual cases and human labels remain future evaluation work.
